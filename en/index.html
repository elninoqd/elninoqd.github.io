Because I came up as a developer, I still read code, review architecture and understand the real cost of every technical choice. Partners get someone who speaks the language of business and stays accountable down to the last line of code.

  • Problem first, technology second. Choose what fits the budget and the stage, not what is trending.

  • Transparent with partners. Raise risks early, commit only to what can be done, and deliver what was promised.

Depending on the problem, we can work through Fractal (full project delivery) or directly with me (per-session consulting, technical review). The exact arrangement is agreed after the first conversation.

Yes. The idea stage is when architecture and technology choices have the biggest impact on later cost.

Is my project information kept confidential?

Yes. I am happy to sign an NDA before going into technical or business details.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I have worked on products for clients in Singapore and the US, in Vietnamese or English, in person or remotely.
